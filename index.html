Heads Up - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Heads Up ("the App") handles user information.

Heads Up is a word-guessing game that allows users to play using built-in word decks and create their own custom decks. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your information private.

1. Information We Collect

Heads Up does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The App does not use a server or backend to collect or store your personal information.

2. Camera and Photo Access

Heads Up may request access to your device's camera or photo gallery when you choose to create a custom deck and add an image.

This access is used only for features you request, including:

  • Taking a photo using your device's camera.
  • Selecting an image from your device's gallery.
  • Adding the selected image to a custom word deck.

Photos and images selected or captured by you are used within the App for your custom decks.

The App does not upload your photos or camera images to any external server.

3. Local Data Storage

Heads Up stores game-related information and custom deck information locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Custom word decks.
  • Images associated with custom decks.
  • Game settings.
  • Scores or game history, if applicable.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

4. Data Sharing

Heads Up does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The App does not use advertising services, analytics services, Firebase, or other third-party services that collect user information.

5. Internet Usage

Heads Up does not require an online account or backend service to provide its core gameplay functionality.

The App's core gameplay and custom deck functionality are designed to work locally on your device.

6. Data Security

Because your custom decks, images, and game-related information are stored locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

We do not receive or store this information on our servers.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data created by the App remains on your device until you delete it through the App's available features or uninstall the App.

Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored App data according to your device's operating-system behavior.

Because we do not maintain a server containing your personal data, there is no separate account or server-side personal data that we need to delete.

8. Children's Privacy

Heads Up does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Since the App does not require an account and does not transmit personal information to our servers, we do not knowingly collect personal information from users.

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of device permissions, including camera and photo access.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be made available through the same Privacy Policy page, with the updated date shown at the top.
